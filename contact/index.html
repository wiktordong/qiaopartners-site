FAQ

Questions clients often ask

How does a Chinese company usually start selling in Europe?

Usually in four steps: confirm the product rules (CE marking and sector rules), decide who imports and sells in the EU (a distributor, an importer or your own entity), price against European competitors, and win the first customers. We cover each step.

What does “you bring the product, we deliver the results” mean?

You provide a competitive product and the budget to grow in Europe. We provide the market knowledge, relationships and execution, and we are measured on customers won, distribution built and sales grown.

Are your people salespeople?

No. They are independent business developers who know their markets, speak the local languages as well as Chinese, and take responsibility for results.

Do we lose control of our products?

No. Under our distribution model your goods stay yours until they are sold. Stock can be held in a customs warehouse in the EU, with duty and VAT paid only when goods are released for sale, and we earn a percentage of what we sell.

Can you help us build a brand in Europe?

Yes. We position your brand for European buyers, localise materials into European languages, and build presence at trade fairs, on LinkedIn and in industry media.

Which products do you work with?

Our main categories are battery energy storage, electric trucks and buses, engines, mining machinery, data centre equipment, biomethane equipment, solar and grid equipment, and aluminium products. We also take on other products with a clear European market.

How are your fees structured?

Distribution is paid as a percentage of sales. Sales and brand work run on monthly mandates with a performance-based fee agreed in writing, and market-entry projects on a fixed fee. The introductory conversation carries no charge.
