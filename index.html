Insights & market intelligence

Understanding the Forces Shaping European Markets.

Regulation, investment and industrial policy are reshaping what Europe buys and from whom. These are developments we follow closely. Each is dated and linked to its source.

Trade & investmentMay 2026

Chinese investment in Europe at a seven-year high

Chinese FDI in the EU and UK reached €16.8 billion in 2025, up 67% on 2024 and the highest level since 2018. Greenfield investment rose to a record €8.9 billion.

Rhodium Group & MERICS, May 2026

EU regulation18 Feb 2027

The battery passport becomes mandatory

Industrial batteries above 2 kWh, EV batteries and light-transport batteries placed on the EU market need a digital passport accessible by QR code.

Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, Art. 77

Industrial policyIn force

Net-Zero Industry Act

Non-price criteria, including resilience and sustainability, now carry weight in renewable-energy auctions and public procurement of net-zero technologies.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1735

Carbon pricing2028

ETS2 starts in 2028

Carbon pricing for buildings and road-transport fuels begins in 2028, one year later than first planned, adding to the case for electrified heat and transport.

ICAP, on the amended EU Climate Law

Mobility2030 · 2040

CO₂ standards for heavy-duty vehicles

Emissions from new heavy-duty vehicles must fall 45% by 2030 and 90% by 2040, driving demand for zero-emission trucks, buses and charging.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1610

Energy2030

35 bcm of biomethane a year

REPowerEU set a goal of 35 billion cubic metres of biomethane a year by 2030, which requires large-scale investment in plants and equipment.

REPowerEU Plan, COM(2022) 230
