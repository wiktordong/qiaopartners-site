**Short answer:** From 18 February 2027, every industrial battery above 2 kWh, EV battery and light-means-of-transport battery placed on the EU market needs a digital battery passport, reached through a QR code. The date is fixed in Article 77 of the EU Batteries Regulation (2023/1542) and does not wait for any further EU act. Chinese manufacturers of BESS and battery packs who sell into Europe without one will not be able to place new units on the market.

## What applies, and when

| Date | Obligation | Who it hits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18 Aug 2026 | Printed label on batteries (Art. 13(1)) | All batteries |
| 18 Feb 2027 | QR code on all batteries (Art. 13(6)); battery passport (Art. 77) | Passport: EV, LMT and industrial batteries above 2 kWh |
| 18 Aug 2027 | Supply-chain due diligence on cobalt, lithium, nickel and natural graphite | Producers placing batteries on the EU market |
| Not yet fixed | Carbon footprint declaration for industrial batteries | Depends on a delegated act still awaited; batteries used only for external storage follow a later timetable |

The carbon footprint rules are where most sources disagree. The passport is due in February 2027 regardless. Fields whose rules are not yet in force are left out until they apply.

## What a Chinese manufacturer should do now

1. **Name your EU economic operator.** The passport is the responsibility of whoever places the battery on the EU market. Decide now whether that is your own EU entity, your importer or your distributor, and put it in the contract.
2. **Build the data once, per battery model and plant.** Chemistry, capacity, manufacturer details, conformity documents and, later, carbon footprint and recycled content. Most of it already sits in your factory systems.
3. **Choose a passport platform early.** Several EU providers host passports and QR codes. Integration with your ERP takes months, not weeks.
4. **Start supply-chain mapping for August 2027.** Due diligence on cobalt, lithium, nickel and graphite needs supplier data from upstream refiners. Ask for it now.
5. **Tell your European buyers you are ready.** A buyer signing a 2027 delivery contract carries the compliance risk with you. Showing passport readiness first is a sales argument.

## Why it matters for Poland and Central Europe

Poland's state-backed storage programme alone funds 172 large projects totalling 14.5 GWh, all due by the end of 2028. Batteries for projects delivered after February 2027 will need a passport.
