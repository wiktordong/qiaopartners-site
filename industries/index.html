Industries

Sector knowledge. European market understanding.

We focus on four clusters where entering Europe is complex, valuable and dependent on local knowledge.

AIndustrial Technology & Advanced ManufacturingBEnergy & InfrastructureCMobility & AutomotiveDDigital & Critical Infrastructure
A

Industrial Technology & Advanced Manufacturing

Automation, robotics, machinery, components and advanced materials for European industrial buyers.

Focus areas

  • Industrial automation
  • Robotics
  • Machinery
  • Industrial components
  • Advanced materials
  • Manufacturing technologies

What European market entry involves

  • CE marking and the new EU Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, applicable from 20 January 2027
  • Distributor and system-integrator networks that control access to end users
  • European expectations on after-sales service, spare parts and local technical support
B

Energy & Infrastructure

Storage, solar, power electronics, grid equipment, biomethane and industrial energy systems.

Focus areas

  • Battery energy storage systems
  • Battery technologies
  • Solar
  • Power electronics
  • Transformers and grid infrastructure
  • Renewable energy equipment
  • Biomethane technologies
  • Industrial energy systems

What European market entry involves

  • EU Batteries Regulation: a digital battery passport for industrial batteries above 2 kWh from 18 February 2027
  • Grid-connection codes, utility qualification and tender requirements
  • Net-Zero Industry Act resilience and sustainability criteria in auctions and public procurement
  • Notification duties under the EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation in larger public tenders
C

Mobility & Automotive

Automotive components, EV supply chains, commercial vehicles and charging infrastructure.

Focus areas

  • Automotive components
  • EV supply chains
  • Commercial vehicles
  • Charging infrastructure
  • Battery components
  • Mobility technologies

What European market entry involves

  • EU CO₂ standards for heavy-duty vehicles: new-vehicle emissions down 45% by 2030 and 90% by 2040
  • Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation (AFIR) requirements for charging networks
  • Supplier qualification and quality-approval processes at European OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers
D

Digital & Critical Infrastructure

AI data-centre infrastructure, cooling, power distribution and industrial digitalisation.

Focus areas

  • AI data-centre infrastructure
  • Data-centre cooling
  • Power distribution
  • Industrial digitalisation
  • Selected technology infrastructure

What European market entry involves

  • Energy Efficiency Directive reporting for data centres of 500 kW and above
  • F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573 rules affecting cooling equipment and refrigerants
  • Security, resilience and supplier-origin scrutiny by critical-infrastructure operators

We bring genuine sector understanding and work with technical and regulatory specialists where deep expertise is required. We do not claim expertise in every technology. Regulatory references were checked on 13 November 2026.
