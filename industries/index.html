Products we work with.

We work with many kinds of products. These are our main categories.

A

Battery Energy Storage

Utility and C&I storage systems, battery cabinets and power conversion.

Typical products

  • Utility-scale BESS
  • C&I battery cabinets
  • Cells and modules
  • Power conversion systems
  • Energy management systems

What selling in Europe involves

  • EU Batteries Regulation: digital battery passport for industrial batteries above 2 kWh from 18 February 2027
  • Grid-connection codes and operator qualification
  • CE marking, safety and fire-protection standards
B

Electric Trucks & Buses

Zero-emission trucks, buses and commercial vehicles, with charging.

Typical products

  • Electric trucks
  • Electric buses
  • Light commercial vehicles
  • Charging infrastructure
  • Spare parts

What selling in Europe involves

  • EU CO₂ standards for heavy-duty vehicles: new-vehicle emissions down 45% by 2030 and 90% by 2040
  • EU type approval for vehicles
  • AFIR requirements for charging networks
C

Engines & Power Generation

Industrial engines, generator sets and power equipment.

Typical products

  • Diesel and gas engines
  • Generator sets
  • CHP units
  • Marine and industrial engines
  • Spare parts

What selling in Europe involves

  • Regulation (EU) 2016/1628 (Stage V) emission limits for non-road engines
  • CE marking under the Machinery Regulation
  • Noise and safety documentation
D

Mining & Construction Machinery

Heavy machinery for mining, quarrying and construction.

Typical products

  • Excavators and loaders
  • Crushers and screens
  • Drilling rigs
  • Conveyors
  • Underground mining equipment

What selling in Europe involves

  • New EU Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, applicable from 20 January 2027
  • Stage V engine emission limits
  • ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU for equipment used in explosive atmospheres
E

Data Centre Equipment

Fibre and power cables, racks, cooling and electrical infrastructure for AI data centres.

Typical products

  • Fibre-optic cables and connectivity
  • Power cables
  • Racks and enclosures
  • Cooling systems
  • UPS and switchgear

What selling in Europe involves

  • Construction Products Regulation: fire-performance class for cables installed in buildings
  • Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU and EMC Directive
  • F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573 for cooling equipment
F

Biomethane & Biogas Equipment

Upgrading, compression and plant equipment for biogas and biomethane.

Typical products

  • Membrane upgrading units
  • Digesters and tanks
  • Compressors
  • Gas treatment
  • CHP units

What selling in Europe involves

  • Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU
  • ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU
  • Grid-injection quality requirements set by gas operators
G

Solar & Grid Equipment

Solar modules, inverters, transformers and grid components.

Typical products

  • Solar modules
  • Inverters
  • Transformers
  • Switchgear
  • Cables and mounting systems

What selling in Europe involves

  • Net-Zero Industry Act resilience and sustainability criteria in auctions and public procurement
  • EU Forced Labour Regulation, applying from December 2027
  • Grid-connection codes
H

Aluminium Products

Extrusions, sheet and foil, building systems and aluminium components.

Typical products

  • Extrusions and profiles
  • Sheet, coil and foil
  • Windows, doors and façades
  • Automotive and battery-enclosure parts
  • Heat sinks and housings

What selling in Europe involves

  • CBAM: from 1 January 2026 only authorised CBAM declarants may import more than 50 tonnes of aluminium and other CBAM goods a year; certificates are bought from February 2027
  • EU anti-dumping duties of up to 32.1% on aluminium extrusions from China (Regulation (EU) 2021/546), under expiry review since March 2026
  • CE marking and declarations of performance for building systems

We also take on other products where we see a clear European market. Regulatory references were checked on 28 November 2026.
