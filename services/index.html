01

Direct Sourcing

We source directly from the producer, with no layers of agents in between. Factories are selected by hand for each request, from manufacturers we know and have worked with, so you get competitive prices and a supplier that fits your specification, volumes and timing.

Request a quotation →

When clients come to us

  • You buy industrial equipment and want to cut out intermediaries
  • Your current supplier is expensive or unreliable
  • You need a specific product and do not know which factory to trust

What you receive

  • Requirement brief: specification, volumes, standards, target price
  • Hand-selected factories and comparable quotations
  • Samples, factory visits and management meetings in China
  • A recommended supplier and offer

How it is structured

Quotations are free of charge. We earn on the trade.

02

Trade & Counterparty Risk

Qiao Partners buys from the producer and sells to you. Your contract, invoice and warranty are with an EU company headquartered in Warsaw, and we carry the counterparty risk towards the factory: payment terms, deposits, delivery and performance. Risk management is our background.

Discuss a purchase →

When clients come to us

  • Your finance team will not prepay an unknown overseas supplier
  • You want EU contract law, EU invoicing and one accountable counterparty
  • Delivery delays or quality issues would be costly

What you receive

  • Contract and invoicing through Qiao Partners in Poland
  • Payment structure and deposits managed with the factory
  • Delivery and performance obligations backed by Qiao Partners
  • Contracts drafted with qualified lawyers

How it is structured

Trade margin included in the price you are quoted.

03

Quality, Certification & Delivery

Before goods leave the factory we make sure they meet EU requirements and your specification: certification, factory audits and pre-shipment inspection with accredited partners. We then handle shipping, customs and delivery to your site, and coordinate after-sales with the producer.

Discuss quality and delivery →

When clients come to us

  • Your product needs CE marking or EU standards
  • You need independent inspection before shipment
  • You want door-to-door delivery without managing freight yourself

What you receive

  • EU certification and documentation coordinated with accredited bodies
  • Factory audit and production capacity check
  • Pre-shipment inspection and quality control
  • Shipping, customs clearance and delivery to site
  • After-sales and spare-parts coordination

How it is structured

Included in the trade, or quoted separately for larger projects.

04

Distributors & Manufacturers

For European distributors we set up regular supply programmes with stable pricing and stock planning. For Chinese manufacturers we find European companies and distributors that need their products, and manage the relationship on the ground.

Discuss a partnership →

When clients come to us

  • You distribute industrial products and want a reliable Chinese source
  • You are a Chinese manufacturer looking for European customers
  • You want a long-term partner, not one-off orders

What you receive

  • Supply programmes and framework agreements
  • Price lists and stock planning for distributors
  • European buyer and distributor development for manufacturers
  • Account management in six languages

How it is structured

Framework agreement or trade margin; for manufacturers, a monthly business development mandate.

Working with qualified specialists

Qiao Partners handles market entry, distribution and commercial work. Legal, tax, regulatory and certification services are delivered by appropriately qualified firms, which we select, brief and coordinate on the client's behalf.

How engagements are structured

Quotations are free. We earn a trade margin that is included in the price you are quoted. For larger projects, inspection or certification work may be quoted separately, and manufacturers can engage us on a monthly business development mandate.

European companies entering China

Our focus is Chinese companies expanding into Europe. We also support selected European companies with market assessment, partner identification and establishment in China.
