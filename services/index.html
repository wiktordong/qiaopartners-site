01

Strategic Advisory & Market Intelligence

We assess the European opportunity for your products: demand, competition, pricing, regulation and market access, country by country. The result is a clear recommendation on whether, where and how to enter.

Request a Market Assessment →

When clients come to us

  • You are deciding which European countries to prioritise
  • You need a realistic view of European prices and competitors
  • Your board expects an evidence-based case before approving investment

What you receive

  • European market assessment and market sizing
  • Competitive intelligence and price benchmarking
  • Regulatory and market-access review, prepared with specialists where required
  • Country prioritisation and market-entry feasibility
  • Go-to-market strategy

How it is structured

Fixed-fee project, typically three to six weeks.

02

Market Entry & Establishment

We design the entry model, whether distributor, partner, direct sales or your own entity, and coordinate the practical steps to operate in Europe with qualified legal, tax and certification specialists.

Discuss Your Market Entry →

When clients come to us

  • You must choose between distributors, partners and your own entity
  • Your products need CE marking or sector-specific certification
  • You want one coordinated plan rather than several separate advisers

What you receive

  • Entry-model development and implementation plan
  • Distributor and partner identification and screening
  • Coordination of European entity establishment with legal and tax partners
  • Coordination of certification and compliance with accredited bodies
  • Local operational set-up support

How it is structured

Market-entry mandate, typically two to six months depending on certification and entity requirements.

03

Commercial Development & Partnerships

We identify and approach European buyers, distributors and commercial partners, arrange qualified meetings and support you through negotiation towards commercial agreements.

Explore a Strategic Partnership →

When clients come to us

  • You have a competitive product but few European relationships
  • Trade fairs and cold outreach have not produced qualified buyers
  • You need support in meetings and negotiations in Europe

What you receive

  • European buyer identification and distributor mapping
  • Qualified business introductions and B2B meetings
  • Commercial partnership development
  • Sales pipeline development and reporting
  • Negotiation support

How it is structured

Monthly business development mandate, usually from month six, minimum six months. A success-based component may be agreed in writing.

04

European Representation & Expansion

We act as your external European business development and strategic coordination partner: representing your company locally, managing partner relationships, coordinating stakeholders and keeping headquarters informed as you expand across countries.

Discuss European Representation →

When clients come to us

  • You need a European counterpart before hiring locally
  • Your partners and customers expect someone on the ground
  • You are expanding from one European market to several

What you receive

  • Ongoing European representation and local business development
  • Partner relationship and stakeholder management
  • Regular local market intelligence and reporting to headquarters
  • Multi-country expansion planning
  • Strategic advisory retainer

How it is structured

Monthly representation agreement or strategic retainer, typically twelve months.

Representation covers the scope set out in the engagement letter. It does not replace a fully staffed European sales organisation unless the engagement is designed for that.

Working with qualified specialists

Qiao Partners provides commercial and strategic advisory. Legal, tax, regulatory and certification services are delivered by appropriately qualified firms, which we select, brief and coordinate on the client's behalf.

How engagements are structured

We work on fixed-fee advisory projects, market-entry mandates, monthly strategic retainers and European representation agreements. Where appropriate, a success-based fee may be agreed in writing. Fees are proposed after an introductory conversation, once the scope is clear.
