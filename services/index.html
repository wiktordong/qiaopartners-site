01

Market Entry & Compliance

We assess demand, prices and competitors for your products, choose the entry and distribution model, and coordinate everything needed to sell legally in Europe: certification, importer of record, entity, customs and tax, with qualified specialists.

Request a market assessment →

When clients come to us

  • You are deciding which European countries to enter first
  • Your products need CE marking, sector certification or CBAM reporting
  • You need an importer of record or a European entity

What you receive

  • Market assessment, pricing and competitor benchmark
  • Entry and distribution model by country
  • Coordination of certification and compliance with accredited bodies
  • Importer-of-record, entity, customs and tax set-up with partner firms

How it is structured

Fixed-fee assessment of three to six weeks, followed by a market-entry mandate.

02

Distribution & Channel Management

We select, appoint and manage distributors, dealers and system integrators across Europe, or act as your appointed distribution partner for agreed countries. We set channel structure, pricing and terms, and coordinate logistics, stock and after-sales with qualified partners.

Discuss European distribution →

When clients come to us

  • You have no distributors in Europe, or the wrong ones
  • Your channel prices and terms differ from country to country
  • You want one partner to run distribution rather than many agents

What you receive

  • Distributor and dealer mapping, selection and onboarding
  • Appointed distribution for agreed countries where it fits
  • Channel pricing, territories and terms
  • Logistics, warehousing and after-sales coordinated with partners
  • Channel performance reporting to headquarters

How it is structured

Monthly distribution mandate or distribution agreement for defined countries.

03

Client & Account Management

We identify and win buyers, manage key accounts locally and in their language, follow orders, claims and service requests, and keep headquarters informed with regular pipeline and market reporting.

Discuss client management →

When clients come to us

  • European customers expect a local contact, not an email to China
  • Orders, complaints and service requests go unanswered across time zones
  • Headquarters lacks visibility of the European pipeline

What you receive

  • Buyer identification and qualified introductions
  • Key-account management and regular customer visits
  • Order, claim and after-sales follow-up
  • Pipeline, forecast and market reporting

How it is structured

Monthly retainer, typically twelve months.

04

Negotiation & Commercial Agreements

We lead or support negotiations with distributors, buyers and tender authorities: price, exclusivity, payment terms, warranty and liability. Agreements are drafted and reviewed with qualified lawyers.

Discuss a negotiation →

When clients come to us

  • A distributor or buyer wants exclusivity, long payment terms or broad warranties
  • You are preparing for a tender or framework agreement
  • Cultural and contractual expectations differ between your team and the buyer

What you receive

  • Negotiation strategy and preparation
  • Leading or attending negotiations in Europe
  • Distribution, supply and agency agreement terms with partner lawyers
  • Tender and framework-agreement support

How it is structured

Per negotiation, or within a distribution or client-management mandate; success fee where agreed in writing.

Working with qualified specialists

Qiao Partners handles market entry, distribution and commercial work. Legal, tax, regulatory and certification services are delivered by appropriately qualified firms, which we select, brief and coordinate on the client's behalf.

How engagements are structured

We work on fixed-fee assessments, market-entry mandates, monthly distribution and client-management mandates, and distribution agreements for defined countries. Where appropriate, a success-based fee may be agreed in writing. Fees are proposed after an introductory conversation, once the scope is clear.
