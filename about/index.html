About us

An independent advisory boutique between China and Europe.

We help ambitious Chinese companies enter, establish and grow across European markets, combining strategic advice with practical execution on the ground.

Who we are

Qiao Partners is an independent China–Europe market entry and strategic business development firm based in Warsaw. We advise Chinese manufacturers and technology companies on European expansion, and we stay involved through implementation: partner selection, establishment, commercial development and ongoing representation.

We are a boutique by design. Every client works directly with senior people, and we bring in qualified specialists only where the work calls for them.

Why we exist

Qiáo (桥) means bridge.

Many Chinese companies now make products that can compete in Europe on quality, technology and price. Fewer have the local relationships, regulatory knowledge and presence on the ground to turn that potential into contracts. European buyers, meanwhile, want reliable suppliers they understand and can hold to account.

We exist to close that gap with substance rather than promises: clear analysis, careful partner selection and consistent follow-through.

Our China–Europe perspective

We understand how decisions are made at headquarters in China and how they are received by European buyers, distributors and regulators. Much of our work is translation in the broadest sense: of expectations, timelines, contracts and business culture, not only of language.

Leadership

Qiao Partners is founder-led. Our founder and CEO has six years of experience working between Chinese and European business and studied economics and finance in Beijing. He leads every client engagement personally.

Professional experience across our team includes Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Tencent and Accenture. These are previous employers of individual team members, not clients or partners of Qiao Partners.

How we work with clients

  • Senior involvementThe people you meet in the first conversation are the people who do the work.
  • Defined scopeEvery engagement starts with a written engagement letter setting out objectives, deliverables, timeline and fees.
  • Evidence firstRecommendations rest on verifiable data and direct contact with the market, not assumptions.
  • DiscretionClient information, strategies and negotiations are treated as strictly confidential.
  • CandourWe report progress regularly and say plainly when a market or a partner is not right for a client.

Our specialist partner network

Market entry touches law, tax, certification, logistics and recruitment. We work alongside qualified firms in each field and coordinate their work, so the client keeps one point of contact. Legal, tax, regulatory and certification services are provided by those specialists, not by Qiao Partners.

  • Corporate and commercial lawyers
  • Tax advisers and accountants
  • Testing, inspection and certification bodies
  • Logistics and customs specialists
  • Recruitment and HR partners
  • Translation and localisation specialists

Our independence

We are not affiliated with any distributor, manufacturer, investor or government body. We do not invest, manage funds or provide investment advice. Our commitment is to the client we represent, on the terms agreed in writing.

Memberships

Qiao Partners is a member of SinoCham, the Polish-Chinese General Chamber of Commerce.
